CPI-Surprise Trading Strategy
2024
Quantitative analysis of inflation-based trading strategy across monetary cycles with rigorous transaction cost treatment
Click to expand →
Overview
Comprehensive quantitative analysis testing a CPI-surprise trading strategy across 20 years (2004-2024). The strategy trades SPY and QQQ based on whether actual CPI inflation comes in above or below market expectations. Analysis includes period breakdown, factor regression, and realistic transaction cost treatment (10 bps per position change).
Key Findings
  • Transaction costs (2.4% annually) eliminate strategy edge in full sample (Sharpe: -0.022)
  • Strategy shows regime dependence: Strong performance in high-inflation 2020-2024 (Sharpe: 0.65+)
  • CPI timing is valuable, but directional prediction adds noise (long-only outperforms directional)
  • No statistically significant alpha after controlling for standard risk factors (p > 0.30)
Methodology
Tested trading signals generated from CPI surprise (actual vs. consensus). Positions entered next trading day with daily rebalancing between equity and risk-free assets. Analyzed performance across full sample and sub-periods. Conducted Fama-French 4-factor regression to isolate alpha. Modeled realistic transaction costs (10 bps per position change).
