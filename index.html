CPI-Surprise Trading Strategy
Quantitative analysis of inflation-based trading strategy with rigorous transaction cost treatment
Click to expand →
Overview
Comprehensive quantitative analysis of a CPI-surprise trading strategy across 20 years (2004-2024). Tests whether trading on inflation surprises generates alpha.
Key Findings
  • Transaction costs (2.4% annually) eliminate strategy edge in full sample
  • Strong performance in high-inflation 2020-2024 period (Sharpe: 0.65+)
  • CPI timing is valuable, but directional prediction adds noise
  • No statistically significant alpha after controlling for risk factors
